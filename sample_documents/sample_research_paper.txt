DocuMind: Efficient Layout-Aware Document Intelligence via Multimodal LLM Inference

Dr. Elena Vance, Dr. Marcus Holloway
Institute for Advanced Machine Learning & Neural Systems

ABSTRACT
Document intelligence systems increasingly rely on Large Language Models (LLMs) to extract structured schema representations from unstructured multi-page documents such as resumes, invoices, and legal agreements. In this paper, we demonstrate a low-latency pipeline integrating layout-aware parsing and high-throughput inference using Groq LPU accelerators. We evaluate performance across extraction fidelity, latency, and token efficiency.

1. INTRODUCTION
Processing semi-structured documents at scale introduces severe bottlenecks in inference latency and token overhead. Traditional OCR approaches discard spatial formatting, while full vision models impose prohibitive compute burdens. Our architecture leverages hybrid text extraction combined with semantic schema validation.

2. METHODOLOGY
Our pipeline operates in three discrete stages:
1. Ingestion & Token Extraction: Robust parsing of PDF, DOCX, and plain-text buffers.
2. Semantic Chunking & Schema Anchoring: Entity classification for targeted extraction (Names, Contact Information, Skills, Experience).
3. Accelerated Inference: Groq-hosted open-weights models delivering structured JSON payloads conforming to strict Pydantic schemas.

3. RESULTS
Across a benchmark dataset of 5,000 resumes and contracts, our method achieved 98.7% entity extraction precision while reducing median response latency to under 400ms.
